# Quickstart: Validating the Game-Style Interface

1. Build (`cd app && ./build.sh`), update `/Applications/MacPlay.app` in place, open from Finder.
2. MacPlay opens on Home: greeting with your first name, hero banner, card rows, right column
   (contract: home-and-theme.md).
3. With the network on, compatible games show Steam artwork; Heartopia-style games without a cover
   show their hero art cropped; turn the network off and reopen: placeholders, no errors.
4. Launch TapTap from My Games, play a few minutes, come back: Running now lists it while it runs, and
   Play time grows (about 30 s resolution).
5. Visit My Mac, Steam, Compatible Games, My Games, My Apps: all use the maroon theme; text readable.
6. Type in the Home search: matching games from all sections appear; Esc or clearing restores Home.
7. Switch to Bahasa Indonesia and Français: new text translated.
